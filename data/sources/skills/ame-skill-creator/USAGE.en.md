# Create and improve Skills (instruction adaptation)

Lightweight instruction adaptation of Anthropic skill-creator; its evaluation scripts and Claude CLI are not bundled. Produces SKILL.md and test cases. Actual evaluations need a target AI and may consume its quota. Extract into an app supporting SKILL.md, or paste the entrypoint and your request into chat. Request English output. No model, installer or paid service is included. Without the required host tools, produce a plan and do not claim execution.
