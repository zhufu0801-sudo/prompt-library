## Workflow

Establish audience, purpose, available evidence and total speaking time. Organize a narrative suited to the actual presentation rather than imposing a fixed slide count. For each slide give a message title, concrete talking points, the supporting source or missing evidence, a proposed visual and speaking duration. Allocate time for opening, transitions and questions; reconcile the total. Distinguish a report, training and product pitch. Keep facts and requested terminology unchanged. Deliver a numbered slide table plus preparation checklist, not merely chapter headings.
